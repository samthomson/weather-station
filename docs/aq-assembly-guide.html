Air Quality Stations — Stripboard Build

30-pin ESP32 · stripboard · sensors in the module directly above the ESP32 · one 15 cm F–M wire per connection

Minimal air station

aq8 · PMS7003 + MQ-135 + MQ-7 · 5 V ≥ 2 A supply (two MQ heaters draw ~300 mA constant)

Stripboard matrix

One row = one net. Every module lands on the row with its own stub. MQ D0 pins stay unconnected. GPIO 35/36 are input-only — safe for the AO outputs. All three sensors ship with male pins — tails plug straight on.

Cables — minimal air station

Three F–M parts per connection, all pluggable and no M–M anywhere: a 15 cm stub (male end soldered into the stripboard row, female socket up), a 30 cm riser (its male end into the stub's socket, female socket up), and a 10 cm sensor tail (its male end into the riser's socket, its female onto the sensor's male pin). The ESP32 skips riser and tail — its stub's female goes straight onto the DevKit's pins.

Maximal air station

aq9 · PMS7003 + SCD41 + SGP41 + BME680 · I²C sensors wire in parallel (no address clashes: 0x62 / 0x59 / 0x77)

Stripboard matrix

One row = one net. Every module lands on the row with its own stub. Solder male pin headers into the SCD41, SGP41 and BME680 boards first — they ship with female holes — then everything uses the same tails as the male-pin sensors. BME680: CS and SDO stay unconnected (board ties CS high for I²C; if the sensor is not detected, bridge CS to 3V3). SGP41: use the 3.3V pin, leave VIN empty.
